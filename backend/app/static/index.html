Features Overview

How Placement Hub Works

• Adaptive AI Questioning: Gemini asks real non-fixed follow-up questions tailored to your previous responses.
• Mic & Camera Processing: Real-time speech analysis (WPM, filler words) and observable camera coaching (posture, eye direction).
• Non-Evaluative Camera Coaching: Camera analysis is purely for coaching feedback and does NOT affect technical scores or job matching.
• TESSA Skill Gap Sync: Directly update your TESSA learning roadmap for detected interview weaknesses with one click.
• Real Job Matching: Live jobs fetched from Adzuna / Tavily with skills-based match scores.
